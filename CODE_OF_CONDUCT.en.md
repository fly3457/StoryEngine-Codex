# Community Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free collaboration
environment for everyone, regardless of age, body characteristics, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or other
identity.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss problems constructively, specifically, and respectfully.
- Respect different creative methods, cultural backgrounds, and levels of technical
  experience.
- Accept reasonable feedback and repair harm when it occurs.
- Address code, documentation, and behavior rather than attacking a person.
- Protect other people's privacy, unpublished manuscripts, and sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, threats, harassment, discrimination, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or unpublished work without
  permission.
- Unwelcome sexualized language, attention, or conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or project resources.
- Other conduct that would clearly be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests, and other
public spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
inappropriate material and may issue warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent
bans for violations.

To report conduct privately, prefer an available GitHub private-contact or reporting
mechanism, or a private channel listed on the repository owner's public profile. Do
not repost a target's personal information in public. Maintainers will protect a
reporter's privacy where reasonably possible and handle reports consistently and in
good faith.
