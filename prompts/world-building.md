# Prompt: World Building

## Instructions for Codex

Phase 2 begins when the user approves PROJECT.md. Confirm that approval from its
recorded state; if clearly given in the current request, record it without asking again.

### Step 1: Read Context

Read PROJECT.md, continuity/changelog.md, any existing world files, and STORY_SEED.md
when it exists. Treat the seed's AUTHORING/fixed world facts and rules as binding
user constraints, its resolved opening values as defaults, and its pressures/outcomes
as possibilities. The seed itself is not Canon or Gate approval. Keep confirmed facts;
log missing files and unresolved choices before creating their content.

### Step 2: Build the World Files

1. world/setting.md: physical world, time period, atmosphere, social structure,
   technology/magic level, and grounding details.
2. world/rules.md: what is possible and impossible, hard rules, conditional soft
   rules, and what characters believe versus what is true.
3. world/timeline.md: relevant history and the story-present timeline.
4. world/locations.md: significant places, sensory details, relevance, and associated characters.

Keep rules usable for the approved premise. Do not introduce a simulation engine or
replace these Markdown files with a structured database. Record decisions in their
owning file, and conflicts with sources in the changelog; do not silently resolve Canon.

### Step 3: Review Gate

Present each world file for review and iterate until approved. Record actual approval,
update PROJECT's World-building status, then proceed to Phase 3: Character Architecture.
