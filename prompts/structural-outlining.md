# Prompt: Structural Outlining

## Instructions for Codex

Phase 4 begins when the user approves the character files.

### Step 1: Read Context

Read PROJECT.md, all world/ and characters/ files, outline/structure.md,
outline/scenes/_TEMPLATE.md, existing act/scene files, and continuity tracker,
threads, and changelog. Read STORY_SEED.md when it exists for its mission, conflict
topology, pressures, reveal paths, and candidate endings; these remain planning inputs,
not accomplished facts or prior approval. Record missing reads. Preserve confirmed
story decisions.

### Step 2: Build Structure and Acts

Populate outline/structure.md with the overall act structure and turning points.
Use the original act-1.md, act-2.md, and act-3.md templates for chapter breakdowns.
Retain the template's story-structure choices; do not confuse story acts with the
fixed eight engine phases. Follow an already-approved structural choice rather than
silently imposing a new one. Give chapters consistent zero-padded filenames.

### Step 3: Create Scene Cards

For each chapter, create the scene cards it needs in outline/scenes/ using the
original template. Include scene ID and chapter/scene numbers, POV, location, time,
word target, purpose, entry hook, 3–5 key beats, emotional trajectory, exit hook,
continuity dependencies and outputs, and threads opened/advanced/closed.
Dependencies say what must already be true. Outputs and planned payoffs remain plans
until written into prose; do not close tracker threads based on an outline alone.

### Step 4: Review Gate

Present structure, act breakdowns, and scene cards for review. Iterate until approved,
record approval in the changelog, update PROJECT's Outline status, and proceed to
Phase 5: Style Calibration.
